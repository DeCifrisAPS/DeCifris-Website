CIFRIS26 - Fourth National Conference

CIFRIS26 is an international event promoted and organised by De Cifris together with Roma Tre University.

The event brings the cryptographic community together in a single place: researchers, practitioners, developers and users meet to share techniques, tools and ideas, and to build the connections from which new collaborations grow.

The programme opens on the morning with a session devoted to institutional and industrial stakeholders working in cryptography and cyber security. The Scientific Session follows, dedicated to research talks, and the associated workshops close the day.

ORGANIZATION

Institution

The “De Componendis Cifris Associazione di Promozione Sociale” (De Cifris) is a non-profit scientific organization whose purpose is to spread cryptography in all its aspects, from theoretical foundations, applications to historical evolution and impact on society, and to facilitate the aggregation of scholars and researchers, so that from their collaboration project ideas and initiatives of scientific interest can rise.
