THE VENUE

Main conference and workshops

Our main venue for the main conference and the PQ, TAC and SYMM workshops is the Rettorato, Roma Tre University, Via Ostiense 133B, Rome.

  • Aula Magna (Tower A, 2nd Floor): plenary and scientific sessions, PQ workshop.
  • Hall C (Tower C, hall): TAC workshop.
  • Aula 2 (Building D, Piazza Telematica, 1st floor): SYMM workshop.

The OPEN and NT workshops take place at the DAMS building, Via Ostiense 139, on the raised ground floor: Aula 3bis for OPEN and Aula 5 for NT.
